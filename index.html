How the Matching Score Works

Transparent evaluation criteria balancing exact term match with contextual relevance.

1. Required-Skill Coverage

Proportion of essential requirements explicitly present in candidate skills and experience sections.

2. Relevant Evidence

Verifies whether claimed skills are supported by surrounding context, metrics, and project descriptions.

3. Lexical Similarity

Calculates term frequency overlap and industry keyword density against the target posting.

4. Semantic Alignment

Evaluates conceptual alignment and domain proximity between your background and the role requirements.

Methodology Notice: All scores generated in this interface are analytical estimations intended for informational guidance. Real applicant tracking systems utilize proprietary parsing engines, and compatibility scores do not guarantee interview invitation or hiring decisions.
