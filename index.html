How It Works

Three straightforward steps to prepare for technical interviews and optimize your resume for recruiters and ATS systems.

  1. 1

    Explore & Follow Roadmaps

    Pick a technology career track, review expected industry skills, and build recommended projects across beginner to advanced milestones.

  2. 2

    Benchmark in Resume Analyzer

    Upload your resume in PDF/DOCX format against target job postings to detect blind spots, missing keywords, and quantify impact.

  3. 3

    Apply with ATS-Friendly Templates

    Format your updated qualifications using verified single-page PDF templates designed to parse cleanly through recruiter screening systems.
